Concours d'Elegance 2025

Activating a Title Sponsorship Through Brand Experience and Stakeholder Engagement

Project
Concours d'Elegance
Location
Nairobi, Kenya
Year
2025
Organisation
Stanbic Bank Kenya
Sponsorship
Title Sponsor
Area of involvement
Brand and Marketing | Event Planning | Stakeholder Engagement

Project Overview

The Concours d'Elegance is a prominent annual motoring and lifestyle event in Kenya, bringing together classic and vintage automobiles, motorcycles, motoring enthusiasts, corporate organisations, partners and members of the public.

The 2025 edition presented an opportunity to bring together brand visibility, corporate engagement and event experience on a significant public platform.

Stanbic Bank Kenya was the title sponsor of the event, placing its brand at the centre of the event's identity and creating an important responsibility to ensure that the sponsorship was reflected through meaningful brand presence, stakeholder engagement and a well-coordinated experience.

During this period, Millimark's founder was part of Stanbic Bank's Brand and Marketing Department, which was tasked with the planning and organisation of the event. The involvement extended across planning and coordination, engagement with the event organiser and external partners, brand visibility, stakeholder engagement and the coordination of the bank's stakeholders on the event day.

The Challenge

Translating Sponsorship into Brand Experience

A title sponsorship involves more than placing a brand name on event materials. It requires coordination across multiple activities to ensure that the sponsoring organisation is appropriately represented and that its relationship with the event is reflected in the overall experience.

For Stanbic Bank, this meant coordinating the bank's participation while working alongside the event organiser and external partners.

Several considerations were central to the process:

  • Ensuring consistent brand visibility across the event.
  • Coordinating with the event organiser and external partners.
  • Supporting the planning and execution of sponsorship-related activities.
  • Facilitating engagement with the bank's guests and stakeholders.
  • Coordinating the bank's stakeholders and related activities during the event.

These responsibilities required collaboration, attention to detail and an understanding of how brand objectives translate into practical event activities.
